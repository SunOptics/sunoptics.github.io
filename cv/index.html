Yifan Sun

Curriculum vitae

I am a theorist working on the nonlinear dynamics of driven-dissipative optical systems: cavity solitons and frequency combs, multimode and spatiotemporal light in optical fibres, and the quantum-limited noise that sets the coherence of both. My work combines bifurcation theory, reduced analytical models and large-scale numerics, and is developed in close contact with the experimental groups that build these cavities and fibres.

OPERA-Photonique, Université libre de Bruxelles · Brussels, Belgium

Methods

What I bring

Theory

Bifurcation analysis and numerical continuation of localised states, breathers and snaking structures; Lugiato-Lefever and generalised nonlinear Schrödinger modelling of driven Kerr cavities and fibres; Variational and collective-coordinate reductions for soliton dynamics and fluctuations; Quantum-limited noise theory: coupled noise channels and timing/phase jitter of cavity solitons; Multimode coupled-mode theory and dimensional reduction for spatiotemporal light bullets

Numerics

Split-step Fourier and pseudo-spectral solvers for one- to three-dimensional propagation; Path-following of steady states with linear stability and eigenvalue spectra; Large-scale spatiotemporal simulation of multimode fibres and parametrically driven cavities; Stochastic simulation of noise-driven soliton dynamics; Python, MATLAB and high-performance computing workflows

Collaboration

Theory-experiment collaboration with fibre, cavity and integrated-photonics laboratories; Independent proposal writing and project management, including two Marie Skłodowska-Curie fellowships as sole applicant; Multi-institution European and international projects across nonlinear and multimode photonics; Invited review and book-chapter writing, and regular conference presentation
